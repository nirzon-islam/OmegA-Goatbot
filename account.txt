FACEBOOK ACCOUNT LOGIN
======================

This file is reserved for Facebook login cookies.

To connect a Facebook account:
1. Export the login cookies for the Facebook account you want the bot to use.
2. Paste the complete cookie JSON into this file, keeping the original JSON structure.
3. Save the file and restart/redeploy the bot so the account session can be loaded.

ALTERNATIVE:
You may provide the Facebook login cookies after deployment through the Agent's account-connection/setup flow, when that option is available. This lets you connect or update the account without editing this file manually.

SECURITY NOTICE:
- Never share your Facebook cookies publicly or commit real session cookies to a public repository.
- Treat cookies like a password: anyone who has them may be able to access your account.
- Use a private repository and restrict access to trusted administrators only.
- If cookies were exposed, log out of other sessions / revoke the affected sessions in Facebook, then generate a fresh cookie set.

No Facebook login cookies are currently stored in this file.
